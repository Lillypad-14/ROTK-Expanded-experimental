ROTK EXPANDED - HERO MECHANICS
==============================

Every hero has a second skill page. Press L or R on the skill tree to switch pages.
From level 21, you get 2 skill points per level.


ARAGORN - Flame of Anduril
  Hitting, stunning and marking enemies fills a green Flame bar.
  Spend the Flame on his new skills to make Anduril blaze.

FRODO - Burden
  The Ring's pull is shown as a bar on the HUD.
  It rises while you fight and falls when you rest.
  More Burden means more power, but it gets dangerous near the top.

SAM - Samwise's Garden
  Plant taters and brambles. They grow, ripen and wilt over time.
  Ripe taters throw themselves at enemies. Pick them up to get Spirit back.

SMEAGOL - Gollum's Grip
  Fighting fills the Grip bar.
  At 100, Gollum takes over for 2 seconds: he hunts enemies and hits for double damage.
  Mash A to take control back early.

GIMLI - The Tally
  Gimli counts his kills.
  His second page is bought with kills as well as skill points.
  Every 10 kills, his next skill is free and hits 50% harder.

GANDALF - The Grey and the White
  Hold R for half a second to switch form.
  Grey: gets Spirit back over time.
  White: loses Spirit over time, but his skills hit 30% harder.
  Each new skill does something different depending on your form.

LEGOLAS - The Glint
  Hold B to draw your bow. After full draw, a glint flashes.
  Let go during the glint for a True Shot (+25% damage).
  True Shots in a row build a streak up to 5.
  His new arrows do their full effect only on a True Shot.

RINGWRAITH (new hero) - Dread
  Scares and stuns enemies. Cannot heal from the usual sources, only through specific mechanics. Killing dreaded enemies or HP absorb gear/abilities.
  Only gets Spirit from kills. Has its own wraith gear.

SARUMAN (new hero) - The Prism
  Every successful colored spell fills one Prism segment, even repeated spells.
  Fill three for a bonus based on the final spell, then your next spell costs half.
  Faster staff attacks restore 2 Spirit when they deal damage.

EOWYN
  Unchanged for now.
